Clinical Guidance

Essential First Aid Awareness

Widely recognized emergency intervention guidelines to stabilize casualties while ambulance teams are in transit.

Medical Disclaimer: For informational and educational awareness only. Never substitute these guidelines for professional medical diagnosis or clinical care.
Injuries Priority: Immediate

Severe Bleeding

Firm, continuous direct pressure with a clean dressing is critical. Elevate limb if fracture is not suspected. Never remove blood-soaked dressings; add more layers over them.

Injuries Priority: Rapid

Burns & Scalds

Immediately cool with cool running tap water for 15-20 minutes. Cover loosely with sterile dressing or cling film. Never apply ice, butter, oil, or toothpaste to burn areas.

Injuries Priority: Moderate

Suspected Fractures

Immobilize the limb in the position found. Do not attempt to force or straighten angled bones. Support joint above and below with soft padding or splints until paramedics arrive.

Medical Emergencies Critical Level 1

Cardiac Emergencies & CPR

Recognize signs: chest tightness, breathlessness, collapse. If unresponsive and not breathing normally, call 108 immediately and administer Hands-Only CPR at 100-120 compressions/minute.

Medical Emergencies Priority: High

Unconsciousness & Airway

Check breathing immediately. If breathing normally, roll into the lateral Recovery Position to keep airways clear and prevent choking. Never give fluids to an unconscious casualty.

Injuries Priority: High

Road Accidents & Collision

Ensure personal safety first. Switch vehicle ignition off and warn traffic. Do not move casualty's neck or spine unless immediate fire risk is present. Call 112 and 108.

Road Etiquette & Civic Duty

Give Way.
Save Lives.

Emergency ambulances transport critical patients where every sixty seconds lost in traffic directly affects survival rates. Creating an unobstructed green corridor is a moral obligation and legally mandated.

The Golden Hour Rule

The first 60 minutes after severe traumatic injury or cardiac episode represents the window where prompt clinical treatment yields the highest probability of preventing death or irreversible damage.

1

Give way safely to approaching ambulances

Check your mirrors, indicate your intention, and pull smoothly to the side of the road when you hear sirens or observe flashing lights.

2

Avoid blocking emergency access routes

Never park on designated hospital access corridors, emergency room driveways, or roadside breakdown lanes.

3

Avoid sudden lane changes & panic braking

Do not slam on brakes unexpectedly. Maintain predictable driving behavior so the ambulance driver can navigate through traffic smoothly.

4

Keep intersections & junctions clear

Do not block pedestrian zebra crossings or intersection yellow box junctions, even during red light congestion.

5

Do not follow ambulances to bypass traffic

Tailgating an emergency ambulance is extremely dangerous, illegal, and obstructs subsequent emergency escorts or support vehicles.

Frequently Asked Questions

Emergency Guidance FAQ

Answers to common public queries regarding emergency calling, ambulance operations, and platform scope.

In India, dial 108 for emergency medical ambulance and trauma dispatch. You can also dial 112, which is the unified national emergency number that directly coordinates with ambulance, police, and fire rescue services. Both numbers are toll-free and accessible 24 hours a day, 7 days a week from any mobile or landline phone without a SIM balance.

When communicating with the emergency dispatcher, remain calm and clearly provide:

  • Exact Location & Landmarks: Building name, street name, nearest landmark or highway exit.
  • Nature of Emergency: What happened (e.g. road accident, chest pain, fall, severe bleeding).
  • Condition of Casualty: Conscious or unconscious, breathing normally or gasping, apparent injuries.
  • Number of People Affected: Helps the dispatcher send sufficient ambulances and personnel.
  • Your Contact Details: Your callback phone number so the responding crew can contact you for directions.
